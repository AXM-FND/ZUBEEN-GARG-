THE MAN · THE MUSIC · THE LEGACY

Zubeen Garg

Born 18 November 1972 in Tura, Meghalaya, and active professionally from 1992 to 2025, Zubeen Garg became one of the defining voices of contemporary Assamese music while working across Indian languages, cinema and live performance.

A LIFE IN MUSIC

He built a life around music — restless, multilingual, rooted in Assam and always moving between folk, film, rock, pop and experimental sound.

Zubeen Garg was born Zubeen Borthakur. Music surrounded him early: he grew up in a culturally and musically inclined family and developed as a singer, composer and multi-instrumentalist. His professional recording career began in the early 1990s, and his Assamese debut album Anamika in 1992 became an important milestone in the modern Assamese music scene.

His work did not stay inside one language or one genre. He recorded in Assamese, Bengali and Hindi and in many other Indian languages and dialects. Apple Music describes him as a singer, lyricist, composer, multi-instrumentalist, music director, scriptwriter and filmmaker; public discography sources document a very large recorded catalogue; exact totals vary by source.

In cinema he moved freely between music direction, playback singing, acting, writing, directing and producing. His national breakthrough came with “Ya Ali” from Gangster (2006). He later continued making Assamese films, including Mission China and Kanchanjangha, while continuing his work as a performer and composer.

ROOTS

Assam first

His career remained deeply connected to Assamese language, culture, folk traditions and Bihu performance, even as his work travelled across India.

CRAFT

Many instruments

He was known as a multi-instrumentalist, working with voice, guitar, percussion, keyboard and other instruments across different styles.

WORK ETHIC

Relentless output

Published accounts describe an unusually prolific recording career spanning more than three decades.

PUBLIC LIFE

Artist & citizen

He used his public voice for cultural causes and social concerns and was also associated with philanthropic and community efforts.

MILESTONES

1972

Born in Tura

Born on 18 November 1972 in Tura, Meghalaya, in a family with strong literary and musical connections.

1992

Anamika

His debut Assamese album marked the beginning of a major recording career.

2000

Music & cinema expand

He entered Assamese cinema more deeply as music director, actor and filmmaker, including work around Tumi Mur Mathu Mur and Hiya Diya Niya.

2006

Ya Ali

“Ya Ali” from Gangster brought his voice to a nationwide Hindi-film audience.

2009

National Film Award

He received the National Film Award for Best Music Direction (Non-Feature Film) for Echoes of Silence.

2017

Mission China

He wrote, directed, produced, acted in and composed music for Mission China, a major Assamese film project.

2019

Kanchanjangha

He again combined filmmaking and music in Kanchanjangha.

2025

The final chapter

Zubeen Garg died in Singapore on 19 September 2025. His music, films and cultural influence continue to be preserved through archives, official releases and public remembrance.

ACHIEVEMENTS & IMPACT

National Film Award

Best Music Direction (Non-Feature Film), for Echoes of Silence (2006), awarded in 2009.

Bollywood recognition

“Ya Ali” became a signature Hindi-film recording and earned a Filmfare Award nomination for Best Male Playback Singer.

Assamese cinema

His film work extended across acting, writing, directing, production and music, with projects such as Mission China and Kanchanjangha.

Cultural influence

His multilingual catalogue and live performances helped carry Assamese music and identity to audiences well beyond the state.
